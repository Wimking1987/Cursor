# 12‑Month Roadmap & Action Plan

A concrete, month‑by‑month plan to get from "we own the machines" to
"profitable within a year." Each phase lists **goals**, **actions**, and the
**KPIs** that tell you it's working.

---

## Phase 1 — Set up & first revenue (Months 1–2)

**Goal:** Be legally operational, produce sellable quality, and land the first
paying jobs.

**Actions**
- Register the Gewerbe; decide Kleinunternehmer vs. regular VAT with a tax advisor.
- Take out Betriebs‑/Produkthaftpflicht insurance; draft T&Cs with reproduction &
  liability disclaimers (non‑safety‑critical parts only).
- Finalise the finishing station (extraction, priming, painting, sanding).
- Dial in material profiles: 2–3 FDM filaments (ASA/PETG/PA‑CF) + 2 SLA resins.
- Build the **scan → CAD → print → finish** workflow on 3–5 personal test parts;
  document the fit‑check protocol.
- Stand up a simple web page + quote form; open eBay/Etsy + one POD platform for
  baseline demand.
- Announce in 3–5 marque forums/groups with a strong before/after post.

**KPIs:** first paid job shipped (≤ day 30); 5 test parts documented with photos;
quote turnaround < 24 h.

---

## Phase 2 — Seed the catalog & prove demand (Months 3–4)

**Goal:** Turn one‑off jobs into repeatable catalog products; hit operating
break‑even.

**Actions**
- Identify the **top 15 most‑requested parts** from forums/clubs; source reference
  samples for the easiest, highest‑demand, non‑safety‑critical ones.
- Publish a **starter catalog of 10–20 proven parts** with real photos, prices,
  and lead times.
- Start billing **prototyping / scan‑to‑CAD** work at €60–90/h — market this
  explicitly, it's your fastest high‑margin revenue.
- Begin a weekly content cadence (one "scan → print → fit" short video/post).

**KPIs:** ≥ 10 catalog parts live; monthly revenue ≥ €1,500 (operating
break‑even ≈ €1,470); first repeat customer; first B2B (workshop) conversation.

---

## Phase 3 — Grow channels & margin (Months 5–7)

**Goal:** Ramp volume across all three revenue streams; reach the
"living‑wage" profitability milestone.

**Actions**
- Sign 1–2 **restoration workshops** as recurring B2B "parts we can't find" clients.
- Partner with 1–2 **marque clubs** (member discount ↔ concentrated demand).
- Add catalog parts continuously (target +5–8/month → compounding passive sales).
- Optimise margin: reduce reprints (QA), templatise quoting, batch prints overnight.
- Review the financial model with real cost data; adjust pricing.

**KPIs:** monthly revenue €4,000–5,600; **EBIT covers a €2,500 founder draw
(≈ month 6–7)**; catalog ≥ 30 parts; return/reprint rate < 5%.

---

## Phase 4 — Scale & de‑risk (Months 8–10)

**Goal:** Break the founder‑time bottleneck and stabilise growth.

**Actions**
- Make the **first hire** (part‑time finishing/packing) once demand is proven —
  this frees founder hours for high‑margin CAD/scan and sales.
- Consider a second FDM printer if machine time (not labor) becomes the limit.
- Systematise fulfilment (packaging SOP, carrier pickup, order tracking).
- Expand POD marketing beyond automotive (makers, small manufacturers).

**KPIs:** monthly revenue €6,500–8,000; **fresh‑cash investment recouped
(≈ month 7)**; consistent 4.8★+ reviews; catalog ≥ 45 parts.

---

## Phase 5 — Consolidate & plan year 2 (Months 11–12)

**Goal:** Lock in profitability and decide the growth path.

**Actions**
- Review full‑year P&L vs. this model; **full invested‑capital payback lands
  around month 11–14** depending on founder draw.
- Decide legal upgrade (UG/GmbH) as liability/turnover grows.
- Choose year‑2 focus: deepen best‑selling marques, license/partner for
  trademarked parts, or expand the POD/prototyping arm.
- Protect and back up the **file library** — it's now the company's core asset.

**KPIs:** monthly revenue €8,700–9,300+; profitable after founder pay;
50+ reproducible parts in the library; documented year‑2 plan.

## Weekly operating rhythm (once running)

- **Daily:** answer inbound quotes (< 24 h), start overnight prints, post 1 photo/story.
- **Weekly:** publish 1 transformation video; add 1–2 catalog parts; review orders & cash.
- **Monthly:** update the financial model with actuals; review margin & KPIs; plan marketing.
