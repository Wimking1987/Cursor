# Founders / Cooperation Agreement (English summary)

> Full binding draft (German, Swiss law oriented):  
> [`de/04-gruendervereinbarung.md`](de/04-gruendervereinbarung.md)

This is a **negotiation draft**, not legal advice. Have a Swiss lawyer / notary review it before signing and before forming a GmbH.

## Agreed commercial terms (as specified)

| Topic | Term |
|-------|------|
| Time commitment | **20 hours per month** per partner (~3 days) |
| Pay in pre-GmbH phase | **Unpaid** (expense reimbursement with receipts only) |
| Trigger | Cumulative project **profit of CHF 20,000** |
| After trigger | Form a **Swiss GmbH** (min. share capital CHF 20,000) within ~90 days |
| Business plan | Living document — may be adjusted; material changes need written consent |

## Profit definition (short)

Revenue minus necessary operating costs (materials, shipping, software, insurance, marketing, etc.).  
**Not** counted as cost: fictional founder salaries. Full definition in the German agreement §5.

## What to fill in before signing

- Full names and addresses of both partners  
- Who owns which machines (Form 3L, X1 Carbon, scanner)  
- Share split for the future GmbH (default draft: 50/50)  
- Bank account for the project cash box  
- Place of jurisdiction in Switzerland  
- Already existing CAD files / IP (Annex A)
